Special balloons and party instructions

Regular balloons: 10 points plus a combo bonus. Golden paw (🐾): Winston Zoomies automatically pops only beneficial balloons for three seconds, never work balloons. Treat (🦴): 50 bonus points. Clock (⏱): adds five seconds, up to a total round length of 90 seconds. Dashed work balloons deduct points and reset the combo: meetings -20, traffic -25, muted microphone -15, red stapler -10, keyboard and mouse -15, alarm clock -30. Negative scores are possible. The bonus stopwatch is beneficial; the alarm clock is a penalty. A missed click resets the combo; balloons floating away do not penalize you. Relaxed Mode ends after 24 pops, with no timer. Work-balloon penalties still apply.
